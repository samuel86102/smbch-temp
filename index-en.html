Our Vision

Orderly
Warm & Welcoming

Shihmen Baptist Church is not just a building, but a community called by God. We are committed to building a warm family-like congregation where everyone can find belonging and grow in an orderly manner through truth.

  • Rooted in Truth

    Through systematic teaching, building a firm foundation for life.

  • Loving One Another

    Experience genuine acceptance and care in small groups.

  • Proclaiming the Gospel

    Going beyond the walls to serve the community and complete the Great Commission.
